1. Decouple: yes. Today each building owns its own goal, and the only thread between buildings is the KPI label (fund/join/trust) on each objective. A town-level tree makes that thread real: town goal → fund/join/trust → sub-goals → key results, edited in one place at the Town Hall, and a building is attached to a subtree (several buildings can share one). 2. Standing vs milestone: the tree needs two node kinds. A standing goal is a direction and is never "met" (e.g. "a funder sees what every token bought"); it keeps generating the next KR. Example: the Bank adjusted-token KR is a milestone of a standing goal; the "Town blockers page" KR is really a standing duty with a schedule.

3. KRs vs KPIs: a KPI is a dial with a live reading (collectives created this month, funders other than you, visitor first sessions that finish work). A KR is a target on a dial by a date, or a concrete thing that will exist in the live town. "Number of new collectives next month" is a good KR under the standing goal "strangers can join".
