# Munder Difflin

Checked 2026-11-05 16:29 UTC (desk clock), via the site and the GitHub API.

## What the sources say
- **What it is (site):** a free, open-source desktop app (macOS, Windows, Linux), a "multi-agent harness", an "office of AI employees" drawn as pixel-art avatars on an office floor (a parody of The Office; "not affiliated with NBC"). Agents are real terminal CLIs (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI; 12 listed) on the user's own subscriptions.
- **Who makes it:** Chaitanya Giri (GitHub `chaitanyagiri`, Bangalore, company "Munder Difflin", 1,098 of the commits; next contributor 27).
- **How agents are orchestrated (site):** an orchestrator, "Michael", takes one sentence, splits it into tasks and assigns them to agents ("clones"); shared task board, per-agent inboxes, agents hand work to each other; crashed agents restart with work intact; persistent memory of who did what and why; one "floor" per project folder; a "circuit breaker" against runaway spend.
- **Humans directing / funding / trust (site):** questions needing a human go to an "Ask me" box. "Keys never leave your machines"; messages between machines signed and sealed; minimal telemetry, off with Do Not Track.
- **Pricing:** Free "$0 forever" (MIT core, unlimited local agents); Pro $20 / 30 days or $150 / 365 days, one-time via Razorpay, one machine at a time; "All payments are final".
- **License / distribution:** MIT (GitHub API). Download from harnessmd.com; also listed on winget (wingetly). Pixel tilesets by LimeZu (commercial licence).
- **Activity (GitHub API, 2026-11-05):** 9,380 stars, 1,322 forks, 279 open issues; created 2026-05-31; last push 2026-11-05 12:23 UTC; latest GitHub release v0.5.5 (2026-10-01); site says 0.5.6.
- **Where it's talked about:** self-reported on site: "GitHub Trending #1", "Product Hunt #5", "100K+ downloads", "1M+ Reddit views", "5M+ Instagram and X". Discord (discord.gg/SEDzP5ZPk5), a blog at munderdiffl.in/blog, a "wall" of testimonials, Peerlist, Trendshift, Hacker News (secondary reports: ~270 points, ~Aug 2026). **The repo runs an automated "seo-engine"**: its latest commits are "seo-engine: day 39 AM, daily brief for 5 Nov 2026, Roo Code and Gemini CLI alternatives" — daily "X alternatives / X vs Y" blog posts.

## What I infer
- Closest peer in *look* to Muster Town (pixel-art workplace of agents with an orchestrator and a task board), but single-user and local: no shared goals, no pooled funding, no public record of decisions.
- Its outreach is deliberate and multi-channel: launch on HN/Product Hunt, Discord, daily SEO comparison posts generated by agents, social video. That last one is something our own agents could do.

## What I could not verify
- All the self-reported reach numbers (downloads, Reddit/X views) and the HN points.
- What exactly Pro's "Stapler", "Capabilities", "Integrations" do.
