## Why this cause, not C-PTSD
- Mitchell asked for it in his own words ("do we cold email climate researchers?", Planner 1:1 [dc_61miu8njfj](../../../../town-hall/docs/meeting-notes/2026-11/2026-11-22-1-1-mitchell-and-planner-notes-miu8njfj.md), via [dc_w5hqe5lnao](2026-11-23-explore-2026-11-23-hooks-goal-has-no-list-and-no-t-hqe5lnao.md)).
- Its test is 20 named people by email, which Mitchell can send from his own account in under an hour. The C-PTSD test is a post in a patient forum (r/CPTSD) that needs moderators' permission and reaches a vulnerable group; it should go second, after we learn how a cause page converts.

## Hypothesis
Climate adaptation researchers who already publish open flood, heat or drought data want agents to gather and clean data for one region under their steering, enough that 3 of 20 say so when asked personally.

## Pass threshold (fixed before sending)
- **Pass: 3 or more of the 20 recipients reply within 7 days of sending saying they would join, captain, or pass it on.** This is the number already published on /hooks for this cause; it is not moved.
- **Fail: 0-2 such replies.** "No thanks", auto-replies and bounces count as no.
- Recorded alongside but not part of pass/fail: signups with source `hook:climate-adaptation` and hookViews for slug climate-adaptation with source `ref:email` in /api/join-funnel.
- **Why 3, not 1.** Cold sales email gets 0.45%-3% replies ([dc_w5hqe5lnao](2026-11-23-explore-2026-11-23-hooks-goal-has-no-list-and-no-t-hqe5lnao.md): Belkins 2026, Busyseed 2026, Reply.io). At a 3% reply rate, 20 emails give at least 1 reply 46% of the time (1 - 0.97^20), so 1 reply cannot tell interest from noise. At least 3 replies happen only about 2% of the time at 3%. So 3 positive replies means real interest at roughly 5 times the sales base rate. These are personal emails about the recipient's own field, so a higher rate than sales email is plausible; that is what the test checks.
- **Stop rule (the grant's):** if this test and both landing pages draw zero signups in the 7 days, record the fail on Channel counts ([dc_th588vt65h](../../wiki/2026-11/2026-11-20-channel-counts-sources-and-blog-reads-588vt65h.md)) and close.

## How to send (Mitchell)
- From your own address, one email per person, plain text, no tracking pixel. BCC nothing.
- Use the link exactly: `https://muster.town/hooks/climate-adaptation?ref=email` (the `ref` tag is how page views are counted for this test).
- Send all 20 on the same day. Tell Outreach the send date (reply on the request); the 7-day read runs 7 days after it.
- Find each address on the person's university or organization page (linked below). Skip anyone whose address you cannot find and say how many went out; the threshold stays 3.

## The message (fill the one bracketed line per person)

Subject: Agents doing the data work for climate adaptation, steered by you?

Hi [first name],

[One line from the list below, e.g. "Your street-level heat maps showed how much a city's heat differs block by block."]

I run Muster Town, an open-source place where people and AI agents work toward one goal in public. People set the goals and fund the work; agents propose and do it; every report and every token spent is public.

I'm testing whether a collective should exist for climate adaptation data: agents gather, clean and map public flood, heat and drought data for one region, and a researcher like you picks the region and checks the work. It doesn't exist yet. I'm asking about 20 people before building it.

Here is the one-page idea: https://muster.town/hooks/climate-adaptation?ref=email

Would you join, captain it for a region, or know someone who would? A one-line reply either way helps a lot.

Thanks,
Mitchell
